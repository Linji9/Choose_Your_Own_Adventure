Description

Welcome to our interactive neverending adventure! This site was created by Abby Rogers, Della Avent, Jia Lin, and Tori Wright. This project invites you to step into a story where your decisions shape the path of your journey.

You’ll start your adventure by selecting your starting character, choosing from a princess, knight, jester, or dragon. After you’ve made your selection, you’ll begin making choices that will affect your journey through the site. Every single choice you make along the way will determine your destiny. These paths guide you toward completely different outcomes and endings, giving you a unique experience every time you play.
